# AI Usage

> [!abstract] These are being asked in 2025–26 backend loops, sometimes by HR, sometimes by the EM in the same breath as design. They want **how you work**, not "AI is the future." Answers later must match what you actually do in Cursor / review / prod — including what you will not paste into a model.

### How do you use AI in your day-to-day engineering work?

### Which tools (Copilot, ChatGPT, Cursor, internal) and for which jobs — generate, explain, test, review, debug, design?

### When do you *not* use AI?

### How do you verify AI-generated code before it hits a PR or prod?

### Tell me about a time AI was wrong (hallucinated API, bad test, subtle bug). How did you catch it?

### Tell me about a time you shipped something that originated from a model. What did you still own?

### Have you used AI on production incidents? What was useful vs dangerous?

### How do you use AI for tests? How do you know the tests are asserting the right thing?

### How do you use AI in code review — of your code and of others' (including others' AI code)?

### A junior pastes a generated patch they do not understand. What do you do?

### How has AI changed your speed? Where has it made you sloppy?

### How do you prompt for backend work (schema, concurrency, failure paths) without accepting the first snippet?

### Do you use AI for HLD/LLD? Where does that break?

### Company code, secrets, customer data, internal URLs: what do you never paste into a public model?

### If we banned AI on your team for a month, what would get worse? What would get better?

### How should a backend team set a policy for AI (review bar, allowed tools, IP)?

### How do you keep learning the underlying idea if a model can emit the boilerplate?

### Describe a refactor or bug hunt where the model wasted your time. What did you change in how you use it?

### How do you handle licensing / "whose code is this" if a model echoes something it should not?

### In this interview process: what did you use AI for, and what did you do yourself?

- Some companies now ask this explicitly. Answer later with the truth.
