# Collaboration, Conflict, and Stakeholders

> [!abstract] People questions. They want a *specific time*, not "I believe in healthy debate." Pick stories where you changed an outcome, not where you were merely present.

### Tell me about a conflict with a peer. How did it end?

### Tell me about a disagreement with your manager.

### Tell me about a time you disagreed on a technical design. What did you do?

### Tell me about a time you disagreed with a PM / product on scope or priority.

### Tell me about a difficult stakeholder. How did you work with them?

### Tell me about a time you had to influence without authority.

### Tell me about a time you changed someone's mind.

### Tell me about a time you compromised. Tell me about a time you refused to.

- Two directions; one story each, not the same anecdote twice.

### Tell me about a time someone else took credit for your work.

### Tell me about a teammate who was not delivering. What did you do?

### Tell me about a time you had to work with someone you found difficult.

### Tell me about a time you gave someone critical feedback.

### Tell me about a time you received harsh or unexpected feedback. What changed?

### Tell me about a communication failure. What did you change after?

### Tell me about a cross-team dependency that slipped. How did you handle it?

### Tell me about a time the team was split and you had to align people.

### Tell me about a time you were the unpopular opinion in the room.

### Tell me about a time you were wrong in a disagreement. How did you walk it back?

### Tell me about a time you helped a struggling teammate.

### Tell me about a time you mentored someone (even informally).

### Tell me about a time you worked with someone much more senior who you thought was wrong.

### Tell me about a time you onboarded someone — or were onboarded badly and compensated.

### Tell me about a time you had to work across time zones / async / another office.

### Tell me about a time you made sure a quieter person was heard, or you were talked over.
