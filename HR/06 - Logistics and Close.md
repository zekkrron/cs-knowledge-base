# Logistics and Close

> [!abstract] Recruiter and closing questions. Not STAR theatre. Still write the true answer later so you do not improvise CTC or notice in the room.

### What is your notice period? Can it move? Buyout?

### When can you join?

### Current compensation. Expected compensation. ESOPs vs cash.

- Also: why that number; flexibility.

### Do you have other offers or processes in flight? Where are you in them?

### If two offers land, how will you choose?

### Any constraint we should know (bond, non-compete, serving notice, background check)?

### Are you interviewing only for backend?

### Is there anything on the resume you want to correct or add?

- Useful if a metric got ahead of you. Decide the sentence *before* they ask.

### What did you think of this interview process?

### Do you have questions for us?

Prep a short list later. Direction, not scripts: team shape, on-call, how they review code, what the first project is, how they use AI internally, what failed in the last six months, how performance is judged. Skip "what is the culture" with no follow-up.

### Anything else we should know about you?

### Why might you turn us down?
