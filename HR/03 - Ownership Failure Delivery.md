# Ownership, Failure, and Delivery

> [!abstract] Delivery questions. Result must include what *you* did and what was true in prod after, not "the team shipped." Incidents are behavioural here; the technical post-mortem lives in Resume notes.

### Tell me about your biggest professional failure.

### Tell me about a time you missed a deadline. What did you do?

### Tell me about a time you overcommitted.

### Tell me about a time you delivered under an unrealistic deadline. What did you cut?

### Tell me about a time you said no (to a feature, a date, or a shortcut).

### Tell me about a time scope crept. How did you handle it?

### Tell me about a time requirements were ambiguous or incomplete.

### Tell me about a time you were blocked. When did you escalate vs keep pushing?

### Tell me about a time you should have escalated earlier and did not.

### Tell me about a time you had to deliver bad news (slip, outage, "this will not make the date").

### Tell me about a production incident you were involved in. What was *your* role?

### Tell me about a bug that escaped to users. What did you change after?

### Tell me about an on-call or off-hours issue. How did you decide what to do?

### Tell me about a time you took ownership beyond your job description.

### Tell me about a project that was cancelled or thrown away. What did you salvage?

### Tell me about a time you were wrong about effort or design and had to reverse.

### Tell me about a process you improved (review, deploy, handoff, docs).

### Tell me about a time you broke a knowledge silo (you were the only one who knew X).

### Tell me about a time you shipped too fast and paid for it.

### Tell me about a time you were too slow / over-engineered and paid for it.

### Tell me about juggling multiple projects or a sudden context switch.

### Tell me about a time you asked for help. Tell me about a time you stayed stuck too long.

### Tell me about a repeated failure (same class of bug/outage) and how you stopped it.
